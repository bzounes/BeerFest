How it works
  1. 1
    Connect & open the app Join the WiFi network and navigate to the address above in any browser.
  2. 2
    Register your beer Tap Add Beer. Enter the beer name, your name, and choose its category. A photo is optional but encouraged!
  3. 3
    Browse the lineup Tap Beers to see everything that's been entered so far.
  4. 4
    Vote Tap Vote, pick a category, then tap a beer to cast your vote. Tap it again to change your mind. 1 vote per category.
  5. 5
    Wait for the reveal The host will announce when voting closes and reveal the winners on the Results page for all to see.
